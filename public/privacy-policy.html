Privacy Policy

Jadpod Fitness Private Limited — Effective Date: July 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Jadpod Fitness Private Limited ("we", "us", "our"), operating under the brand name FitFuel, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our meal subscription service delivered through WhatsApp and related platforms.

By using FitFuel's services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and delivery address provided during the subscription process.
  • Location Data: Delivery location and address details used to coordinate meal deliveries.
  • Payment Information: Payment transaction details processed through Razorpay. We do not store your credit/debit card numbers, UPI IDs, or banking credentials on our servers.
  • Communications: WhatsApp messages exchanged with our automated ordering system, including order preferences, meal selections, and feedback.
  • Session Data: Temporary session identifiers stored in Redis with automatic expiry (24-hour time-to-live) used to maintain your conversation state with our chatbot.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your meal plan subscriptions and individual meal orders.
  • Coordinating delivery schedules and communicating order confirmations, meal changes, and notifications via WhatsApp.
  • Processing payments through our payment processor, Razorpay.
  • Managing your subscription, including plan changes, cancellations, and expiry reminders.
  • Improving our service quality and customer support.
  • Complying with legal obligations where applicable.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share your information only with the following third-party service providers as necessary to operate our service:

  • Razorpay: Payment processing. Razorpay handles payment card and banking data in compliance with PCI-DSS standards. We only receive transaction confirmation and order IDs.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): Message delivery for order confirmations, meal notifications, and chatbot interactions via the WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • Petpooja: Restaurant POS integration for order dispatch and fulfillment. We share order details (items, quantities, delivery time) with Petpooja to prepare and dispatch your meals.
  • Supabase: Database hosting and management for customer profiles, subscriptions, and order records.
  • Upstash: Redis-based session storage for maintaining chatbot conversation state.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

  • Customer profiles and subscription records: Retained for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to support order history and legal compliance.
  • Order records: Retained for operational and accounting purposes.
  • Session data: Automatically deleted after 24 hours of inactivity via Redis time-to-live (TTL) expiry.
  • WhatsApp messages: Subject to Meta's data retention policies for the WhatsApp Business API.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay; no sensitive financial data passes through or is stored on our servers.
  • Session data is stored in encrypted Redis instances with automatic expiration.
  • Access to administrative functions is protected by authentication credentials.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.
  • Opt-out: You may stop receiving WhatsApp notifications by messaging us with a stop/unsubscribe request.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Our service uses minimal cookies necessary for session management within the admin panel. We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics tracking on our public-facing pages.

9. Children's Privacy

FitFuel's services are intended for individuals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
